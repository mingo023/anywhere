Support

Anywhere on your iPhone or iPad pairs with Anywhere on your Mac. The two talk over Tailscale.

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/mingo023/anywhere/issues. Install, the agents the Mac app runs, and the phone app are in the README.

Pairing

  • Install Tailscale on the iPhone and the Mac, and connect both. The phone app does not include Tailscale.
  • On the Mac, open Anywhere → Settings → Phone → Pair a phone. The QR code lasts 5 minutes.
  • On the iPhone, scan that code. Or choose Enter code instead, and type the Mac's Tailscale address and the code. Leave the port out and the app uses 4517.
  • If the phone can't reach the Mac, check that Tailscale is connected on both and that the Mac is awake.

Privacy
